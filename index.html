Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About PromptBox

Explore how our curated AI prompt library, live variable replacer, and direct AI integrations work.

What is PromptBox and how does it help creators & developers?

PromptBox is a modern, curated AI prompt generator and copy-paste hub containing over 80 production-tested prompts across YouTube thumbnails, 2D village animations, Midjourney character art, Hindi moral stories, web development, and social media growth. It eliminates trial-and-error by providing structured prompt engineering templates ready for immediate production use.

How do the dynamic variable replacement fields work on prompt cards?

Each prompt template features customizable placeholder brackets such as [Topic], [Style], or [Character]. You can edit these values directly in the input fields on the card or in the modal. As you type, the prompt text updates in real-time, and copying or launching will automatically use your customized values.

What does the direct "Try in ChatGPT" button do?

Clicking "Try in ChatGPT" automatically copies your customized prompt to your system clipboard and opens ChatGPT in a new tab with your prompt pre-filled in the prompt bar, enabling you to generate results with zero extra clicks.

Which AI models and platforms are supported?

PromptBox offers specialized prompts calibrated for OpenAI ChatGPT (GPT-4o), Midjourney v6 and Niji 6, Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, AutoDraft AI, Runway Gen-3 Alpha, Sora, and DALL-E 3. You can filter the library by your preferred tool using the Tool dropdown filter.

Can I save custom prompts or submit my own templates?

Yes! Click the "+ Submit Prompt" button in the navigation header to add your own prompts with custom categories, target AI tools, bracketed variables, and pro tips. All submitted prompts and favorite bookmarks are automatically saved to your local browser storage (localStorage) and persist across refreshes.

Are the prompts free to use for commercial and client projects?

All prompts in PromptBox are 100% royalty-free and open for personal, commercial, and client work. You are free to generate YouTube videos, client codebases, game assets, marketing campaigns, and published articles using these prompts.
